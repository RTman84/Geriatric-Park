## 2.2 Own-Elder training: Tinker's Workshop and the Lodge ("Hunker Down") - PLAN
Idea: Workshop adds EXP for the player's own Elders; the Lodge lets a player place one of their own Elders in their own park to gain experience, alongside friends' Elders (Resident Exchange already does XP for Elders placed in friends' parks).
- **Mechanic:** "Training slots". The owner places an Elder in a slot at the Lodge (Hunker Down: slow, safe XP) or the Workshop (Apprenticeship: faster XP, small Materials cost per hour). While training the Elder is "away" (reuse the existing `awayUntil` lockout: not on squad, not in Arena/Raid/PvP, not scrap-able), and XP is paid on collection. XP per hour = base rate x building level multiplier x `xpCatchUp(level)` so it still matters at the endgame; max 8 hours counted per session, one claim per Elder per day cap.
- **Slots:** Lodge starts with 1 slot, +1 at levels 5 and 10 (max 3); Workshop 1 slot, +1 at level 10 (max 2). Existing perks (Lodge: visitors 3 + ceil(level/2), +6% guest XP per level; Workshop: -3% gear upgrade cost, +5% salvage) stay.
- **Numbers to start with (tune with real data):** Lodge 15 XP/hour/level-weighted, Workshop 30 XP/hour with a 5 Materials/hour cost; daily cap per Elder = what one 8-hour session pays. No PP, no passive income (standing rule). Record in ECONOMY.md.
- **Build:** client-only to start (new save field `training: { elderId, where, startedAt }[]`; cloud save already syncs game state); UI in `WorkshopPanel.tsx` and the Lodge window (`ExchangeOverview.tsx`); a Mailbox note on completion is optional. Clock-forward cheating is possible on a client-only timer (same as producers today); XP is not money, but a server clock is the later hardening if it matters for ladders.
- **Effort:** about 1 session. **Tests:** pure-function test for XP/hour and caps, headless check of the slot UI.

## 2.3 Friend chat that blocks slurs and inappropriate words - PLAN (recommendation: do NOT ship free-text chat at launch)
Verdict: sensible for a social game, but risky and expensive for a solo developer at launch. Google Play's user-generated-content policy expects, for any chat/UGC: content filtering, in-app report and block, and ongoing moderation; it also raises the content rating ("users interact") and adds "Messages" to the Data Safety form and privacy policy. The player base includes minors (13+). A filter cannot catch everything (spelling tricks, context), so free text always needs a human report path.
- **Phase A - ship at launch (safe): "Quick Chat".** A fixed list of about 20 friendly phrases and a few stickers (Nice game! / Rematch? / Good morning! / Bingo! / Thanks for visiting!), sent through the existing Mailbox. No free text, no moderation burden, no policy change. About half a session.
- **Phase B - after launch, only if wanted: filtered friends-only text.** Asynchronous Mailbox-style messages (no real-time), friends only (never strangers), short length, rate limits, server-side filter (word list with normalization for leetspeak/spacing, slur list, links and contact info blocked), **Report** (stores the message in a Supabase `reports` table for review) and **Block/Mute** per friend, a remote kill switch, privacy policy + Data Safety + content rating updates, and an admin way to review reports. About 3-4 sessions plus ongoing moderation. Never build rooms or global chat.

## 2.4 "OG Posse": player-made groups tied to factions - PLAN (recommendation: YES, but after launch)
Verdict: it belongs in this game (Arenas and factions already create the group fantasy, and groups drive retention), and the faction rule is a good fit. It is too much BEFORE the launch blockers (Play test, release build, review), so schedule it after the first closed test is running. This replaces the earlier "Alliance/Clan, someday" item.
- **Rules:** a player creates a Posse (name 3-20 chars through the same word filter, short tag, banner from a preset art set) and must choose one of the three factions; every member must be in that faction (leaving the faction means leaving the Posse; the existing 30-day faction-switch lock applies). Size 20 at first. Roles: Owner, up to 2 Co-leads, Members; invite by friend code or request, no free-text bio or chat (Quick Chat phrases only).
- **What a Posse does (all rewards are Tickets / Materials / TV Dinners / XP / cosmetics, never PP or passive income):** a Posse screen (roster with power, level, titles); weekly Posse Points counted server-side from members' Arena, Raid and PvP participation (reuse the board counters); a weekly shared Posse Goal (for example 500 combined Raid hits) paying a capped reward through the Mailbox; Posse leaderboard per faction and overall; faction-vs-faction weekly standings (sum of Posse Points) with a faction title for winners; optional small bonus to Arena dues for stationed Posse members. Per-member daily caps so alt accounts cannot farm it.
- **Data/API:** migration 031 `posses`, `posse_members`, `posse_invites`, `posse_week_points`; `api/posse.ts` (create, invite, accept, leave, kick, promote, info, lazy weekly settlement like the boards); Mailbox kinds `posse_invite` / `posse_reward`; Tutorial card; ECONOMY.md section; add the new tables to `api/account/delete.ts` (delete the player's membership; if the Owner deletes their account, promote a Co-lead or dissolve). 
- **Safety:** name filter + Report on Posse names, Owner kick, 13+, no chat. **Effort:** 4-6 sessions (data and API 2, UI 2, art and polish 1-2). **Art needed:** Posse banner emblems (6-9 icons) and a Posse screen header.
