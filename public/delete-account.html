Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Geriatric Park account and its data yourself, at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  • Open Geriatric Park and sign in.
  • Tap the settings gear to open Settings, and find Account & Display Name.
  • Tap Delete account, type DELETE, and tap Delete forever.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. The app then resets to a fresh start on your device.

Can't open the app?

Email ReisunAbel@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us which email and sign-in method you used). We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and sign-in details).
  • Your cloud save and game progress, profile and display name.
  • Your friends and friend requests, Mailbox messages, park and building data.
  • Your Arena and Raid records, Court ladder entries and honors, leaderboard and weekly board scores.
  • Your Mementos balance and records. Unspent Mementos and unredeemed items are lost.

What is kept

  • Purchase, ad-view and revenue records that we need for accounting and legal reasons. They are kept without your name or account: they are no longer linked to you.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups until they expire on the provider's normal schedule. We do not restore deleted accounts.
  • Anything Google holds in its own systems (for example Google Play purchase history, or ad data) is governed by Google's policies and can be managed in your Google account.

Purchases already made cannot be refunded by deleting your account. To request a refund, use Google Play's refund options.

Only want to remove local data?

In the app, Settings has a "Reset local data on this device" button. It clears the save on that device only and does not delete your account.
