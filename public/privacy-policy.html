🧓 Geriatric Park

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Geriatric Park ("we", "our", "us") is a map-based game for Android and the web. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. By using the game you agree to it. If you do not agree, please do not use the game.

Last updated: October 14, 2026.

2. Information we collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address and how you sign in (email and password, an emailed link, or Google sign-in). If you use Google sign-in we receive your Google account email from Google; we do not receive your Google password. You may also choose a display name. You can play on a single device without an account, in which case your progress stays on that device.

Game data (cloud save). When you are signed in, your game progress is saved on our servers so you can restore it on another device: level, Elders, items, buildings and park decorations, currencies (Pension Points, Tickets, TV Dinners, Building Materials, Mementos), quest and pass progress, settings, and similar game state.

Social and competitive data. To power friends, battles, Arenas, Raids, leaderboards and ladders, we store your display name, friend code, level, selected title and icon, squad power, the Elders you feature, your park and building levels, your friend list and requests, Mailbox messages, scores, and honors. Other players can see your display name, level, title, icon, featured Elders, park and rankings. You can choose to be hidden from the "Find Players" list with the "Show me in Find Players" switch on the Friends screen.

Location. With your permission, the game reads your device location to place items, Elders, buildings and Arenas on the map around you. Your precise coordinates stay on your device and are not stored on our servers. When the map loads nearby Arenas, the game sends the identifiers of the map areas (grid cells of roughly 2 km) it needs, which reveal only your approximate area. We do not keep a history of your locations. You can turn location off in your device settings; the game will not be able to show the map around you.

Advertising and device information. The Android app shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and for personalization subject to your device's ad settings. The website may show Google AdSense ads, which use cookies. When you watch a rewarded ad, we record that the ad was completed (your account, the time, and the resulting reward) in our revenue records.

Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive and store a purchase confirmation (product, time and a purchase token) so we can grant the item and handle refunds. We never receive your payment card details.

AI-generated text. Some flavor text (such as battle quotes or Elder bios) is generated by Google's Gemini API. Only game content such as an Elder's name, type and action is sent for this. Your account details and location are not sent.

Technical logs. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and request) for security and reliability.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game: saving progress, showing the map, friends, battles, Arenas, Raids, leaderboards and the Mailbox.
  • To provide purchases, rewards and the ad-funded Community Reserve and to prevent cheating and abuse.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.
  • To show ads and measure their performance (through Google).

We do not sell your personal information.

Pension Points and the other in-game currencies are virtual game items. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

4. Who we share information with

We use these service providers, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase – account sign-in and our database (stores accounts and game data).
  • Vercel – hosting for the website and our server functions.
  • Google – AdMob and AdSense (advertising), Google Play (purchases and distribution), Google sign-in, and the Gemini API (AI text).

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of players or the game. Other players see only the social information described in section 2.

5. Cookies and local storage

The website and app store your save, settings and sign-in session in your device's local storage. The website's ads may use cookies set by Google. You can clear local storage or cookies in your browser or device settings; clearing local storage removes unsynced progress on that device.

6. Children

Geriatric Park is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and game data until you delete your account. You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app: Settings, then "Account & Display Name", then "Delete account". You can also read the steps, or request deletion by email, at /delete-account.html.

When an account is deleted, we permanently delete your sign-in account, cloud save, profile, friends, Mailbox, Arena, Raid, ladder, leaderboard and Mementos records. We keep purchase, ad-view and revenue records that we need for accounting and legal reasons, but they are disconnected from you and no longer show your account. Copies held in our hosting provider's backups expire on that provider's normal schedule. Data held by Google (for example ad and purchase records in Google's own systems) is governed by Google's policies.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data (see section 7).
  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.
  • Turn off location in your device settings at any time.
  • Hide yourself from the "Find Players" list (Friends screen, "Show me in Find Players").
  • Control ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads) or at Google Ads Settings.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and our database is only reachable through our own server functions, which check who you are before reading or changing your data. No online service can be perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will tell players in the game. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
