Updated 2026-10-18. Keep this file in the repo root so every Claude account can read it.
Supersedes the art sections of the 10-16 / 10-17 carry-over docs. Replaces ART_PROMPTS.md sheet-by-sheet.

HOW TO USE
1. Open the next sheet below, copy EVERYTHING inside its box (style + layout + items are already one paragraph).
2. Paste the NEGATIVE PROMPT into Leonardo's negative field (same for every sheet).
3. Attach one finished, good-looking sheet (for example a Common antique sheet S15) as a Style Reference at low-medium strength.
4. Generate, pick the best, upload it and tell me the sheet id (for example "S20").
5. I cut it, check it on a DARK background (the game is dark), fix halos, export webp and wire by item NAME.
If one cell is bad, tell me the number. I can drop that item, and I will list it for a re-roll.

NEGATIVE PROMPT (paste for every sheet):
text, letters, numbers, watermark, photo, realistic, 3d render, blurry, thin lines, pastel, flat shading, items touching, cropped, label pill, caption, banner, drop shadow, cast shadow, floor, table, extra handles, extra legs, duplicated parts

ERRORS TO LOOK FOR BEFORE UPLOADING (these are the usual AI mistakes):
- double handles or two heads on a cane / cue / spoon; extra legs or uneven rungs; nonsensical spokes or planks
- readable gibberish text on posters, clock faces, radio dials, typewriter keys, certificates
- white halo, white gaps inside items, or a grey shadow smudge under items
- two items merged or touching; an item cut by the cell edge
- bowling or other things that do not exist in our game (we have shuffleboard, bingo, gardening, potlucks, canes, porch life)
